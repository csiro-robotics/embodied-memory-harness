# Preconditions
- The target object is not immediately visible.
- A light source (e.g., desklamp) is available and can be used.

# Steps
1. Use desklamp to illuminate the area.
2. Search the illuminated area for the target object.
3. Take the target object from its location.
4. Examine the object under the light.

# Pitfalls
- The desklamp may not be sufficient to illuminate the area.
- The target object may be obscured or not in the illuminated area.
- The robot may fail to locate the object even after illumination.

# Not established
- Whether the desklamp is the only effective light source available.
- Whether the object is located in a different room or area not illuminated by the desklamp.
