# Preconditions
- The robot must be trained to recognize objects and their locations.
- The robot must be able to distinguish between objects and avoid confusion.

# Steps
1. Take <obj> from <place> with <tool>
2. Move <obj> to <destination> with <tool>
3. If <obj> is not found, check multiple receptacles systematically.
4. Consider alternative locations based on object type and task requirements.
5. Use <tool> to examine or manipulate <obj> if necessary.
6. Confirm the location of <obj> before proceeding with further actions.

# Pitfalls
- Confusing similar-looking objects (e.g., tissuebox and box).
- Failing to check multiple receptacles when the object is not immediately visible.
- Retaking objects from the destination instead of completing the task.

# Not established
- The exact locations of all objects in every room.
- The effectiveness of object recognition in low-light or cluttered environments.
