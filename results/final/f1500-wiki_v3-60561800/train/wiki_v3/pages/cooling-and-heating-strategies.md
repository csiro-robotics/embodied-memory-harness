# Preconditions
- The target object must be identified and located before any action is taken.
- The object must be correctly picked up and not confused with similar-looking objects.

# Steps
1. take <obj> from <place>
2. heat <obj> with <tool>
3. move <obj> to <destination>
4. take <obj> from <place>
5. cool <obj> with <tool>
6. move <obj> to <destination>

# Pitfalls
- Confusing the target object with visually similar items (e.g., mug with cup, potato with tomato).
- Failing to locate the target object, leading to repeated searches and incorrect actions.

# Not established
- Whether the robot can distinguish between objects with high visual similarity without explicit labeling.
- Whether the robot can reliably identify objects in cluttered or poorly lit environments.
